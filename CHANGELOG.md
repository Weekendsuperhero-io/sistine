## [Unreleased]

### Changed
- **Day follows the hue: the backing under sheer glass, the tonal crystal gloss and the tint wash take a share of their own hue's sRGB ceiling.** Flat caps sized for the tightest hue held the hues that peak light to a fraction of what they can hold near white. The backing (`--glass-solidify-bg`, L92) is now `min(c × 1.36, 92% of its hue's L92 ceiling)`, on its own scale `--glass-solidify-c-scale` so the opaque card is unchanged (it took 65% of the preset's L88 opaque cap, 0.036 where none is pinned: green, yellow and teal jewels sat at 32–51% of the ceiling, the status tints and frescoes at 26–56%); the tonal gloss (L97) is capped at 95% of its hue's ceiling instead of a flat 0.013 (lapis's), which held peridot, citrine, aventurine and turquoise at 19–31%; and the day wash, which nothing capped (a custom colour asked up to twice its ceiling and clipped, celestite 105%, the neon-city fresco 110%), is capped at 95% of its hue's L72 ceiling, with each preset whose wash sits at another lightness pinning its own 95% (24 pins; the L72 fit would grey lapis to 63% of its wash). Each cap is a hue-following fit every tint scope evaluates (`--glass-wash-hue`, the wrapped hue the night cap already read, is now declared for both modes), generated by `scripts/fit-hue-cap.mjs` (the night wash's generator, taking a profile: `night-wash`, `day-backing`, `day-gloss`, `day-wash`, and `pins`), which regenerates the night cap byte for byte, finds each ceiling's breakpoints itself (L92 has a kink on a flank, not only maxima), and exits non-zero if a fit passes its share anywhere. Measured: the gloss of green, yellow and teal themes, status tints and frescoes carries 1.7–5× the colour (peridot 0.013 → 0.065) and their backing up to 3.3× (success 0.036 → 0.120); the default glass's backing 0.015 → 0.024. The ten blue, violet and red jewels and the info tint, whose backing sat at 94–99% of the ceiling under their pinned opaque caps, come down to 91–92% (−3% to −8% chroma, an OKLab ΔE under 0.004), and nine presets whose wash asked 95.1–95.5% of the ceiling are trimmed to 95% (−0.1% to −0.6%). Body text keeps its floor plus margin everywhere by day (`check-contrast`: 14 presets move, aventurine 82.3 → 78.5, the rest −0.1 to +1.3; the tightest is still destructive at 77.1). Moonstone is exempt and keeps its cream: it keeps the backing and gloss formulas it had (on its own page and inside another preset's), so none of its day values moves. A custom colour that asked past its ceiling now paints at 95% of it with its hue kept; Chromium had clipped it to a more saturated colour with its hue and lightness drifting, so a custom blue (h268) reads calmer than it did. **Night is unchanged, value for value**: `tests/night-snapshot.test.ts` pins every custom property the theme computes at night on 267 scopes (every preset, gloss flavour, page style and material, presets nested in each other, custom colours on `<html>` and nested) to a snapshot taken before this change; the night rule restates the night values of the day caps at every night scope, and a scope nested in a dark page keeps the gloss cap around it (`inherit`), as it did. `lib/oklch-utils` gains `HueCapTable`, `hueCap`, `DAY_BACKING_CAP` / `DAY_GLOSS_CAP` / `DAY_WASH_CAP`, `dayBackingCap` / `dayGlossCap` / `dayWashCap` and `GLASS_SOLIDIFY_C_SCALE_DAY` (replacing `GLASS_WASH_C_MAX_DAY`); AutoForeground, the foreground tester, `glassSurface` / `glassSolidSurface`, `check-contrast` and `check-theme` follow. `check-gamut [guard]` sweeps all four caps at their surface's lightness from −720 to 1080, a ten-thousandth of a degree apart near every breakpoint, and now also sweeps the backing itself, which a nested `var()` default had kept out of the scrape; `tests/vibrant-day.test.ts` holds the CSS equal to the mirrors and each preset's backing and gloss to their hue's share.
- **Deep night is the default: at night every tint's wash, the tonal gloss and the border go deep, so white text reads on dark tint.** One new mode token, `--glass-night-depth` (100 on `:root`, 35 on `.dark`), and the engine paints the tint wash (and lustre's) and the tonal gloss at the darker of their own lightness and it: `min(var(--glass-wash-l), calc(var(--glass-night-depth, 100) * 1%))` and `calc(min(var(--glass-gloss-l, 94), var(--glass-night-depth, 100)) * 1%)`. Every scope that sets a tint recomposes it, so presets, frescoes, status tints, custom colours and previews all go deep, and a preset already deeper keeps its own (Onyx's wash, 16). Before, the night wash stayed at 58 or at each preset's own day lightness (aventurine 88, citrine 91, peridot 92), and white text on the wash itself measured 1.2:1 (peridot) to 7.6:1 (lapis), under 4.5:1 on 21 of the 25 tinted presets; at L35 it measures 10.9–12.5:1. On the composited sheer surface, white text goes from 5.8–11.7:1 to 8.8–13.5:1, and the tightest night body text in `check-contrast` from 77.7 Lc (sodalite) to 79.1 (tourmaline). Day and light mode are unchanged, by value, on every preset: `tests/deep-night.test.ts` pins the light wash, lustre wash, tonal gloss, border, input edge, accent, secondary and muted fills, focus rings and the fills' text of all 28 scopes to a snapshot of the tree before deep night, and `check-contrast`'s light rows are identical.
  - **The night wash's chroma cap follows the hue.** At L35 sRGB holds 0.058 (h200) to 0.235 (h264) of chroma, and asking `c × 2.5` there would clip 24 of the 25 tinted presets, 1.16× (tanzanite) to 2.42× (turquoise) over. The wash takes `min(…, var(--glass-wash-c-max, 0.4))`, and at night `--glass-wash-c-max` is the L35 ceiling itself as a function of `--glass-tint-h`, evaluated by the browser per tint scope with `calc`/`min`/`max` alone: the ceiling is three convex arcs between three cusps, so each arc is the max of a few tangent lines (every tangent of a convex arc lies under it), cut off at its cusps by lines falling 200 per degree (24 tangents; `scripts/fit-night-wash-cap.mjs` generates them). The blue cusp is a step, 0.210 → 0.235 at h264.1348, and its cut starts 0.00023° past it, so the cut must fall the whole step in that distance (110 per degree at the least): a first cut at 20 passed the ceiling by up to 8.7% in the thousandth of a degree under it. It takes ANY hue: a tint hue is not always 0–360 (the harmonic hues are the theme's plus up to 270, and a consumer tints scopes with them, so theme 250 puts a tool card at 430, whose night wash a first cut greyed to chroma 0). `mod()` would wrap it but needs Chrome 125 against the floor's 120, so `--glass-wash-hue` wraps it into (−180, 180] with `atan2(sin h, cos h)` (Chrome 111) and reads the angle back as a number through a small-angle sine, times 572958 rather than 1800000/π: Lightning CSS, in the build, rounds the long one to exactly that, which moved the shipped cap off the checked one by up to 6.9e-4 at the blue cusp, so the rule is written in numbers the minifier keeps (a test minifies it and holds every number as written) and the source, the mirror and the checks evaluate what ships. A floor of 0.057, under the tightest ceiling anywhere on the circle, sits under the fit. It holds 92.4–99.5% of the ceiling at every whole degree from −720 to 1080 and never passes it, sampled to a ten-thousandth of a degree within 0.01° of each cusp (`check-gamut [guard]`), and 98.7–99.5% at every preset's hue, within 1.3% of a per-preset pin, so no preset pins one (the first cut pinned 25 and gave every other scope a flat 0.057 guard: a custom blue at h268 kept a quarter of its 0.227, and a one-step chroma drag off lapis, which makes it a custom tint, dropped its wash from 0.2125 to 0.057; it now stays 0.2125). `lib/oklch-utils`' `nightWashCap` mirrors the fit, held equal to the CSS at every tenth of a degree and every one of those cusp samples, so AutoForeground models it on the DOM path and on a drag's snapshot alike. Nothing declares the cap by day, and the 0.4 fallback binds nothing there.

- **Still scenes cost no compositing layers.** Measured in Safari Technology Preview's WebKit at 1440×900, on top of the page's own: synthwave and moonrise still 25 → 0, aurora still 7 → 0, clouds / dune / forest / peaks / meadow still 6 → 0, and a 13-swatch picker of previews 0 (it was 50 with synthwave and moonrise drawn last, 109 without). - **Aurora by day**: the day palette is now BLUE HOUR so the lights show by day. Under the old pale golden-hour sky (L 0.76–0.85 below the zenith, plus a white cirrus veil and a horizon haze) the curtains could only sit below the sky's lightness and read as muddy bands, and the whole sky read washed out; now the upper sky is a saturated twilight blue (zenith L 0.42) and the curtains sit above it in lightness and glow, as they do at night. The band stops short of L 0 / L 100 on the high-contrast end, which costs no readability and keeps the tint from washing out to pure black or white at one edge. `rampAxisColors` is untouched, so the docs swatches still show the full range.
- **Conic gradients had a hard seam**: a conic wraps, so whatever sits at 360° butts straight into 0°. An open ramp met its own opposite end at the twelve-o'clock line and drew a hard edge through the wallpaper, at full range, white against black (measured seam ΔL = 1.0). Conic now closes the loop: the cyclic `hue` axis repeats its first color at 360°, the rest mirror out and back, and the center plateau is dropped since it reads as a lopsided wedge once bent into a circle. Seam ΔL/ΔC/ΔH now measure 0. Frescoes bypass the ramp, so they get the same clamp and loop-close applied directly.
- **Tint chroma went uncontrolled on the extreme surfaces**: sRGB's chroma ceiling depends on BOTH lightness and hue, so one `--glass-tint-c` scalar could not land equally across 17 presets. Two places asked far past what any hue can hold and let the browser's gamut mapping decide the result: the near-white sheet/border/crystal stack at L 95–100% (lapis delivered 9% of its declared chroma there where peridot delivered 98%), and `--glass-gloss-ink`, whose ×4.25 multiplier is calibrated for the default tint and reached `C=0.6375` on a preset, roughly double the maximum chroma sRGB holds at *any* hue. Added `--glass-tint-c-hi` (`min(var(--glass-tint-c), 0.025)`) for the 13 near-white declarations and clamped the gloss product to `0.3`. The default tint (0.018) sits under both and is unchanged; measured border chroma across every preset is 0–0.0015 either way, which matches the engine's own note that the sheet is too sheer to show a hue, the color comes from `--glass-tint-wash`, which is untouched. This is the CSS-side counterpart to the 3% margin `lib/oklch-utils` already carried for the computed path.

### Fixed
- **Popper observers were rebuilt on every render**: the six popper surfaces composed their refs with `ref={composeRefs(ref, snapRef)}` written inline, which hands React a new callback identity each render. React responds by detaching the old ref (calling it with `null`) and attaching the new one, and that detach runs `useSnappedPopper`'s cleanup, so the `MutationObserver` watching the popper wrapper was torn down and rebuilt on *every* render of *every* popper. Added `useComposedRefs` (same shape as Radix's own: `useCallback` over the composed callback with the refs as the dependency array) and moved all **8** call sites across the six components onto it. Kept local rather than importing `@radix-ui/react-compose-refs`, since these ship through the registry and a direct dependency would have to be declared on all six.
- **`DialogContent` gains `bodyClassName`**: the inner scroll wrapper added for the overflow fix forced `grid gap-4` onto every dialog body, and `className` targets the outer surface, so consumers had no way to reach it. `bodyClassName` styles the wrapper: swap the grid for flex, drop the gap, or take the scrolling over entirely.
- **Colour picker default ignored keyboard users**: the themed hex was applied on `onPointerDown`, which never fires for someone tabbing to the swatch and pressing Space/Enter; they opened the picker on a stale value. Moved to `onFocus`, which precedes activation on both paths.

### Security
- **PR review workflow no longer inherits every secret**: `.github/workflows/pr-review.yml` used `secrets: inherit` on a job that triggers on `synchronize`, so every push to a PR branch ran the reusable workflow with the repository's full secret set. Now passes only the three the review agents actually need, `JULES_API_KEY`, `JULES_PR_CLIENT_ID`, `JULES_PR_PRIVATE_KEY`, explicitly, so adding a secret to the repo cannot silently widen this workflow's blast radius.
- **Reusable workflow pinned to a SHA**: it referenced `platform-tools/.github/workflows/reusable-pr-review.yml@main`, a mutable ref that runs with this repository's token and secrets; any push to that repo's default branch would execute here unreviewed. Pinned to `72bbd12`, with the branch and date kept as a trailing comment.
- **Popper surfaces scaled from their centre**: `popover`, `tooltip`, `select` and `hover-card` play a `zoom-in-95` entrance but never set `transform-origin`, so they grew from their own middle instead of from the trigger that opened them. Each now reads Radix's own `--radix-<component>-content-transform-origin`, matching what `dropdown-menu` and `context-menu` already did. `select` sets the variable only in `position="popper"` (its default); under `item-aligned` the variable is undefined, `transform-origin` computes to its initial `50% 50%`, and that mode keeps centre-scaling, which is what it wants.
- **Reduced motion**: the looping animations outside the pattern scenes now honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: the skeleton shimmer stops, spinners slow rather than freeze (a frozen loader reads as hung), and the opacity-only `pulse` is deliberately left alone. The pattern scenes additionally release `will-change` when motion is off, instead of holding a promoted compositing layer for an animation that isn't running, the speed control's **Static** setting releases it too.

### Added
- **Declared browser support**: `browserslist` in `package.json` (Safari 17.5+ / Chrome 120+ / Edge 120+ / Firefox 128+), derived from the features the theme actually uses rather than chosen: `text-wrap: balance` sets the Safari floor, bare-`&` CSS nesting and unprefixed `mask-composite` set Chrome's, `@property` sets Firefox's. ### Changed
- **Motion easing**: added `--ease-out` / `--ease-in-out` / `--ease-drawer` to the theme (usable as `ease-out`, `ease-in-out`, `ease-drawer`) and pointed `--default-transition-timing-function` at the ease-out curve. Nothing in the library used an explicit `ease-out` before, so every bare `transition` ran on Tailwind's symmetric default, which eases *in* as well, delaying the first frame, the one the user is watching. There is deliberately no `ease-in` token. `sheet` moves off `ease-in-out` (wrong for a panel that enters and exits) onto `ease-drawer`, and narrows its `transition` to transform and opacity so the glass `backdrop-filter` is no longer interpolated on every open.

- **Outrun sun on every day horizon**: `clouds`, `dune` and `aurora` now show synthwave's banded sun low on the horizon in light mode instead of a small high disc, and hand back to the moon at night. - **`@tanstack/react-table` v8 → v9**: migrated the `data-table` block to v9's feature architecture: `useReactTable` → `useTable`, and the row models moved out of table options into a `tableFeatures()` registry (`columnFilteringFeature`, `rowSortingFeature`, `rowPaginationFeature` plus their `filteredRowModel` / `sortedRowModel` / `paginatedRowModel` slots). `getCoreRowModel()` is gone: the core row model is implicit. Types now take `TFeatures` first (`ColumnDef<typeof features, Member>`), `table.getState()` is `table.state`, and sort/filter functions are registered by name and referenced per column rather than auto-detected. Only the three features this block uses are registered, so the rest tree-shake out.
